# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Dashboard Highlights

* **Core KPIs Analyzed:**
  * **Total Sales Amount:** 5.02M (50.2% of 10.0M target)
  * **Total Quantity Sold:** 25.36K (84.5% of 30.0K target)
  * **Average Unit Cost:** 2.48K
  * **Average Unit Price:** 2.73K
* **Time-Series Analysis:** Tracked monthly sales performance from Jan 2023 to Jan 2024.
* **Regional & Category Breakdown:** Compared revenue contributions across North, East, West, and South regions alongside core product categories (Clothing, Furniture, Electronics, Food).
* **Salesforce Performance:** Evaluated individual sales representative results (David, Bob, Eve, Alice, Charlie) broken down by region.
* **Customer Segmentation:** Analyzed revenue splits between New vs. Returning Customers.
* **Dynamic Slicers:** Built interactive filters for Region, Customer Type, Product Category, Payment Method, Sales Channel, and Date Range.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

* **Top Performing Region:** The **North Region** generated the highest total sales volume at 1.4M.
* **Customer Retention:** Returning customers contributed over 50.4% of overall sales volume compared to 49.6% from new customers.
* **Category Drivers:** Clothing and Furniture led overall regional revenue, contributing 1.3M each to the grand total.
* **Top Sales Representative:** **David** emerged as the top-performing sales rep, generating 1.1M in total sales across all regions.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

* **Looker Studio:** Interactive dashboard creation and visual report design.
* **Microsoft Excel / CSV:** Data cleaning, dataset structuring, and raw transaction logs (`Sales_Looker_Studio_Dataset.csv`).
* **Business Intelligence & Analytics:** KPI tracking, trend forecasting, and performance metrics.
